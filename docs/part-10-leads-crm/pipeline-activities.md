# Activities, Tags & Lead Tracking

Never lose track of a prospect with comprehensive activity tracking and contact history.

---

## Managing Activities

Inside any lead card, team members can schedule:
* **Tasks:** Internal todos with deadlines.
* **Calls:** Scheduled phone calls with automated reminders.
* **Meetings:** Demo presentations and customer reviews.
* **Deadlines:** Contract expirations or payment due dates.

---

## Lead Tagging & Filtering

Tag leads with custom color-coded badges. The advanced filter drawer allows filtering by:
* Assigned Sales Representative
* Associated Business Number
* Minimum Deal Value ($)
* Activity Status (Overdue, Due Today, Completed)
