# Lead Management & CRM Pipeline

inVAS includes a native, full-featured **CRM Pipeline** designed to convert WhatsApp conversations into revenue.

---

## Pipeline Views

* **Board View (Kanban):** Drag-and-drop deal cards across custom sales stages (e.g., *New*, *Contacted*, *Proposal Sent*, *Won*, *Lost*).
* **List View:** Dense spreadsheet-style view with filtering, bulk actions, and CSV export.
* **Calendar View:** Shows scheduled follow-up calls, meetings, and activity deadlines chronologically.

---

## Custom Deal Stages
Administrators can rename stages, assign target conversion probabilities, and view live pipeline dollar values calculated dynamically per column.
