# Omnichannel Inbox & Navigation

The inVAS Chatting Inbox is the core workspace where customer care agents, sales managers, and supervisors interact with customers in real time.

---

## Navigating the Inbox

The interface is structured into three clear panels:
1. **Left Panel (Contact List & Tabs):** Shows active and waiting chats, unread badges, search filters, and customer service window countdown timers.
2. **Center Panel (Conversation Thread):** Displays the message stream, message delivery statuses, interactive reply buttons, media previews, and the smart composer.
3. **Right Panel (Contact Intelligence):** Displays the customer's phone number, assigned agent, tags, custom attributes, quick lead creation button, and conversation notes.

---

## Chat Ready vs. Awaiting Conversations

To prevent customer inquiries from falling through the cracks, inVAS organizes conversations into dedicated status tabs:
* **Chat Ready:** Conversations with an active Customer Service Window where agents can type free-form replies.
* **Awaiting:** Contacts who are waiting for an agent assignment or where the 24-hour window has expired and requires a re-engagement template.

---

## The Customer Service Window Timer

Every conversation card displays a dynamic countdown timer (e.g., `18h 42m`):
* **Green:** More than 12 hours remaining.
* **Orange:** Less than 4 hours remaining (priority response alert).
* **Expired:** Window closed; composer switches to template selection mode.
