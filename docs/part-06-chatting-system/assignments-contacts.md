# Chat Assignments & Contact Management

Collaborative team workflows ensure high-volume customer inquiries are evenly distributed and monitored.

---

## Chat Assignment Modes

1. **Manual Assignment:** Agents or supervisors can assign any active conversation to themselves or team members via the dropdown in the chat header.
2. **Round-Robin Automatic Assignment:** Distributes incoming chats sequentially among all online, available agents.
3. **The "Is Assignable" Toggle:** In team settings, administrators can mark specific team members as non-assignable (e.g., billing managers or auditors).

---

## Contact Management in Chat

* **Assigning Labels / Tags:** Add tags like *VIP*, *Hot Lead*, *Invoice Pending*, or *Complaint* to filter contacts later.
* **Pinning Conversations:** Pin up to 5 critical conversations to the top of your contact list.
* **Blocking & Blacklisting:** Block spam callers or abusive numbers with a single click. Blocked numbers are prevented from triggering bots or reaching agents.
