# Cloud Call Center (Premium)

The inVAS Call Center transforms distributed teams into a carrier-grade contact center with WebRTC telephony.

---

## Roles & Access Control

* **Agent Workspace:** Minimalist calling panel, active queue position indicator, customer caller ID, and call transfer buttons.
* **Manager / Supervisor Console:** Live queue monitoring, agent availability boards, call barging, and whisper coaching.

---

## Queue Status & Call Routing

* Inbound callers enter ACD (Automatic Call Distribution) queues.
* Smart routing distributes calls to available agents based on idle time.
* If all lines are busy, callers hear customizable Music-on-Hold.

---

## Call Recordings & Legal Hold

* All calls can be automatically recorded in stereo.
* Recordings are indexed in the **History & Recordings** archive.
* **Legal Hold:** Administrators can flag sensitive recordings with Legal Hold status to prevent automatic storage purge cycles.
