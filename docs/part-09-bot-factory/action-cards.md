# Bot Factory: Action Cards & Workflows

Action cards perform backend operations quietly in the background without requiring user input.

---

## Key Action Cards

<div class="doc-image">
  <img src="/images/pdf/page_80_img_1.png" alt="Add to Phonebook Card" />
  <div class="caption">Figure 9.6: Adding customer to targeted Phonebook action node</div>
</div>

* **Add to Phonebook:** Dynamically enrolls the user into a specific phonebook for future segmented broadcasts.
* **Add to Label:** Applies customer tags (e.g., *Interested in Tier 2*, *Spanish Speaker*).
* **Add to Leads (CRM):** Automatically converts the conversation into a new Deal card in your sales pipeline.
* **API Request Card:** Performs HTTP calls to external servers (fetching order statuses, validating accounts, or writing to external CRMs).
* **Transfer to Human Agent:** Gracefully exits the bot and alerts human agents in the inbox.
