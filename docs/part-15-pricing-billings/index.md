# Platform Pricing & Billings

A transparent breakdown of inVAS subscription plans, Meta conversation fees, and call pulse calculations.

---

## Billing Components

1. **Software Subscription:** Monthly or annual seat license covering platform access, Bot Factory, inbox, and CRM pipeline.
2. **Meta WhatsApp Platform Charges:** Direct carrier-grade conversation rates determined by Meta based on message category and user country code.
3. **Voice Call Pulses:** Calculated in 6-second increments for outbound WhatsApp phone calls.

---
