# Template Pricing & Meta Charges

WhatsApp messaging pricing operates on a per-conversation model determined by Meta rate cards, supplemented by inVAS platform markup fees.

---

## How Pricing Works

Every template broadcast initiates a billable **24-hour conversation window** in that category:

1. **Meta Conversation Charge:** Determined by:
   * **Category:** Authentication, Utility, or Marketing (Marketing commands higher rates; Utility and Authentication are lower).
   * **Destination Country Code:** Rates vary by country (e.g., North America, Europe, Middle East, Africa, Asia).
2. **inVAS Platform Markup:** A fixed marginal throughput fee per message sent through your inVAS workspace.

::: tip Customer-Initiated Inquiries Are Free
When a customer messages your business first, non-template responses sent within the 24-hour customer service window are completely free of Meta conversation fees.
:::
