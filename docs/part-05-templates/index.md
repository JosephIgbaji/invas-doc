# WhatsApp Templates Overview

WhatsApp message templates are standardized, pre-approved messages used to initiate conversations with customers or respond outside the 24-hour Customer Service Window.

---

## Why Do Templates Require Approval?

Meta mandates template review to maintain a spam-free ecosystem:
* Protects users from unwanted, low-quality marketing blasts.
* Ensures businesses correctly categorize their messages for appropriate billing.
* Enforces strict guidelines against deceptive language, prohibited goods, and phishing.

---

## Template Lifecycle Workflow

```
1. Create Draft ──> 2. Submit to Meta ──> 3. AI / Review Check ──> 4. Status: Approved ──> 5. Launch Campaign
```

Templates can contain rich media headers (images, documents, video), dynamic variable placeholders (`{{1}}`, `{{2}}`), and interactive call-to-action or quick-reply buttons.
