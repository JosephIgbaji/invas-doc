# Template Approval & Quality Ratings

Understanding template validation criteria and quality maintenance prevents service disruptions and account penalties.

---

## Common Reasons for Template Rejection

1. **Incorrect Category:** Submitting a promotional discount under the Utility category (Meta will automatically reclassify or reject it).
2. **Missing Variable Samples:** Submitting placeholders like `{{1}}` without providing realistic example values.
3. **Floating Variables:** Putting placeholders without surrounding context (e.g., a message body consisting only of `{{1}}`).
4. **Grammar & Spelling Errors:** Major typos or formatting violations.
5. **Shortened or Deceptive URLs:** Using URL shorteners (e.g., bit.ly) instead of full domain URLs.
6. **Prohibited Content:** Mentioning cryptocurrencies, gambling, weapons, alcohol, or adult products.

---

## Template Statuses

* **Pending:** Under review by Meta's validation systems (typically takes between 2 minutes and 24 hours).
* **Approved:** Ready to send in campaigns, bots, or direct agent chats.
* **Rejected:** Failed validation. Check the feedback tag in inVAS to correct and resubmit.
* **Paused:** Temporarily halted by Meta due to repeated negative customer feedback.
* **Disabled:** Permanently blocked due to poor quality scores over time.

---

## Template Quality Rating

Meta evaluates every template based on how customers react when receiving it:
* 🟢 **Green (High Quality):** High open rates, low block/report rates.
* 🟡 **Yellow (Medium Quality):** Average feedback; safe to continue sending.
* 🔴 **Red (Low Quality):** Multiple recipients reported or blocked the message. At high risk of being paused or downgraded.
