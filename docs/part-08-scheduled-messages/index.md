# Scheduled Messages & Automated Reminders

Automate future message delivery without human intervention using the **Scheduled Messages** module.

---

## Schedule Frequencies

* **One-Time:** Send a single message at a specific future date and time (e.g., tomorrow at 09:00 AM).
* **Daily:** Repeat message every day at a set time (e.g., daily clinic intake reminders).
* **Weekly:** Trigger on designated days of the week (e.g., every Monday morning status update).
* **Monthly:** Send once per month on a specific calendar day.
* **Yearly:** Trigger annually (e.g., customer birthday greetings, policy renewal notices).

---

## End-of-Month Dynamic Trigger

Months have varying day counts (28, 29, 30, or 31 days). The **End-of-Month Trigger** solves this:
* Automatically calculates and triggers on the exact final calendar day of each month regardless of leap years or 30-day months.
* Essential for subscription renewals, monthly statement deliveries, and payroll confirmations.
