# Introduction to inVAS

**inVAS** is an enterprise client communication and engagement platform built natively on the **WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API)**. As an official Meta Business Partner solution, inVAS provides organizations with carrier-grade tools to connect with customers through WhatsApp at scale—combining conversational messaging, drag-and-drop bot automation, omnichannel customer support, CRM pipeline tracking, and cloud call center capabilities into one unified workspace.

---

## What Can You Do with inVAS?

inVAS transforms WhatsApp from a simple one-to-one messaging app into a full-scale customer relationship and revenue channel:

* **Customer Support & Team Inbox:** Multi-agent inbox with automatic routing, customer service window countdowns, conversation tagging, and canned responses.
* **No-Code Bot Automation:** Build intelligent interactive bots using a visual drag-and-drop canvas (Bot Factory) to qualify leads, answer FAQs, handle bookings, and trigger external APIs.
* **Targeted Campaigns & Broadcasts:** Send personalized marketing promotions, transactional alerts, and account notifications using pre-approved WhatsApp templates with built-in rate chunking.
* **Built-in CRM & Deal Pipeline:** Track customer journeys using Kanban boards, assign lead stages, record call/chat activities, and monitor sales values.
* **E-Commerce Catalog Integration:** Connect your Meta Commerce Manager catalog to let customers browse products, view variants, and place orders directly in WhatsApp chats.
* **Voice Calling & Cloud Call Center:** Inbound and outbound WhatsApp voice telephony with WebRTC floating panels, queue management, call recordings, and supervisor whisper modes.

---

## Who is inVAS For?

inVAS is engineered for businesses of all sizes seeking to automate and scale customer engagement:

1. **E-Commerce & Retail Brands:** Driving automated sales, product browsing, abandoned cart recovery, order updates, and delivery notifications.
2. **Customer Service & Support Teams:** Replacing dispersed WhatsApp numbers with a centralized multi-agent dashboard with SLA timers and role permissions.
3. **Financial Services & FinTech:** Sending secure authentication codes (OTPs), transaction alerts, statement requests, and account verification messages.
4. **Healthcare & Clinics:** Managing appointment bookings, automated reminders, and client inquiries.
5. **Telecoms & Enterprise Carriers:** Deploying white-labeled omnichannel contact centers with high-concurrency throughput.

---

## Prerequisites for Getting Started

Before connecting your WhatsApp Business account to inVAS, ensure you have:

* **Meta Business Portfolio (formerly Facebook Business Manager):** An active Meta Business account with admin rights.
* **Dedicated Phone Number:** A phone number that can receive SMS or voice calls for one-time verification.
  ::: tip Important Number Requirement
  The number **must not** be currently registered on the standard WhatsApp consumer app or WhatsApp Business mobile app. If it is, you must delete the account from the mobile app before linking it to the Cloud API.
  :::
* **Business Verification (Recommended):** While you can start in a sandboxed tier immediately, verifying your business in Meta Business Manager unlocks higher messaging tiers (up to unlimited daily conversations).
