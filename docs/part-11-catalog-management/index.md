## Connecting Your Catalog

1. In inVAS, open **Catalogs** and click **Continue with Facebook**.
2. Select the Meta Business Portfolio hosting your e-commerce catalog.
3. Grant permissions for inVAS to view and manage catalog products.
4. Select the active catalog to link with your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA).
